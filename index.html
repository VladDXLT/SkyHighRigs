ALT 500 FT · Before you jump

Rental policies

Waiver required

Every renter signs our rental agreement and waiver before pickup or shipping.

Reserve ride policy

If the reserve is deployed, a reserve repack fee applies no matter who repacks it. On long-term rentals you can get it repacked and keep jumping, but we repack it again when the rig comes back.

Damage & cleaning

Gear comes back the way it left. Damage and cleaning fees apply to mistreated gear.

Experience & sizing

Canopies are matched to your exit weight and experience. We may ask for your license, jump numbers and USPA membership.

FAQ

Do I need an account to see prices?

No. All inventory, availability and pricing is visible without signing up.

Can I rent just a canopy or just a container?

Yes. In the rig builder choose "I have my own container" or "I have my own canopy" and only pay for what you need.

Where do I pick up gear?

Pickup at Skydive Perris or Skydive Elsinore. We also ship nationwide and worldwide.

How do I pay?

Send your booking request through the site. We confirm availability right away and send you a secure payment link.

Can I buy the rig I rented?

Yes. Most rental gear is also for sale. Ask us and we'll credit part of your rental toward the purchase.

What if I'm between sizes?

Message us with your height, weight and jump numbers and we'll help you pick the right setup.
